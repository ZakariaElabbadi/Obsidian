- What should you consider when deciding on the right data visualization?
    
- What is your first step in determining the best data visualization for a presentation?
when deciding on the right visualization i think about what type of data i have and what message i want to show. i also think about who will see it because a simple audience need a simple chart and a technical audience can understand more complex ones.
the first step for me is to understand what question i am trying to answer with the data. once i know the question i can decide if i need to show a comparison or a trend or a relationship and then choose the chart type that fit best for that.

- What are some advantages and disadvantages of creating a chart directly in a spreadsheet?
    
- How did this activity build your knowledge of spreadsheets and data visualization?
one advantage of making a chart in a spreadsheet is that it is very easy and fast because the data is already there. but one disadvantage is that the chart options are limited and it is not as powerful as tools like Tableau so you cant make very complex visualizations.
this activity help me understand how to connect data to a chart and see how changing the data change the chart automatically. it also show me that even simple tools like spreadsheets can make useful visualizations and this give me more confidence to work with data in different ways.

- What did you learn about your presentation delivery? Was anything more or less difficult than you expected?
    
- What worked well in your slide deck? What could you improve?
i learn that speaking clearly and at a good speed is more hard than i thought. when i record myself i notice that i sometimes talk too fast and don't give enough time for the audience to look at the charts before i move to the next slide.
i think the structure of my slide deck work well because it follow a clear order and each slide have one main idea. but i think i can improve by adding less text on each slide and using more visuals because too many words can make it hard to read and listen at the same time.

- If you were the data analyst involved, how would you respond to your stakeholders?
    
- What would be the impact of not addressing these objections?
if i was the data analyst i would listen carefully to the objection and try to understand what the stakeholder is worried about. then i would explain my analysis clearly and show the data that support my conclusions so they can see that the results are based on real evidence and not just my opinion.
if i don't address the objections the stakeholders might not trust my work and they can make wrong decisions based on their own assumptions instead of the data. this can also damage my reputation as an analyst because people will think i am not able to defend my findings or handle feedback in a professional way.
